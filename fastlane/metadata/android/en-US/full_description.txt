Pariyojana (परियोजना) is a sovereign, 100% offline-first personal operating system and deep research command center built for developers, cybersecurity researchers, students, and ambitious creators.

Designed with local-first 256-bit SQLCipher encryption and zero-knowledge architecture, Pariyojana gives you complete data sovereignty with zero tracking, zero paywalls, and 120 FPS fluid physics.

Key Feature Modules:

⚡ 1. QUICK-CAPTURE IDEA VAULT
• Instant audio/voice capture with live waveform visualizer and speech-to-text.
• Rich Markdown editor with live preview, syntax highlighting, and tags.
• Instant search with fuzzy indexing across all stored knowledge.

📊 2. 6-STAGE SCRUM & KANBAN ENGINE
• Full project lifecycle tracking: Backlog → Grooming → In Progress → Review → Blocked → Done.
• Subtask checklists with automatic completion progress calculation.
• Priority tags (Low, Medium, High, Critical) and deadline countdowns.
• Skeuomorphic file/folder tree navigator with multi-level nesting.

🔬 3. DEEP RESEARCH & DOSSIER HUB
• Live ArXiv Paper Explorer: Search millions of peer-reviewed computer science, AI, and math preprints.
• BibTeX citation manager with 1-click clipboard export.
• Tech News & Threat Radar: Integrated Hacker News and Cyber Security advisory feed with silent caching.

💼 4. JOB APPLICATION & SALARY TRACKER
• 5-Stage Job Pipeline: Saved → Applied → Shortlisted → Interview → Offer.
• Smart Salary Intelligence Engine with real-time Take-Home & Net In-Hand calculations for Indian (INR) and US (USD) tax regimes.
• Resume & portfolio attachment binder with secure document storage.

• Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) multi-provider AI hub (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek, Ollama, Perplexity, OpenRouter).

🔒 ZERO-KNOWLEDGE SECURITY ARCHITECTURE
• 256-Bit SQLCipher AES Database Encryption at rest.
