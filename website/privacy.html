🛡️ Privacy Policy for Pariyojana

100% Zero-Knowledge & Offline

Last Updated: August 17, 2026 | Effective Date: August 15, 2026
Developer / Organization: Cyber_buddy | Package: com.navii.pariyojana

1. Executive Privacy Commitment

Pariyojana is engineered with a strict principle: Your data belongs exclusively to you. It is a 100% offline-first, locally encrypted personal operating system for ideas, Kanban projects, research papers, and job applications. We do not sell, harvest, monetize, or transmit your personal data to any private or third-party servers.

2. Local On-Device Encryption & Data Storage

  • SQLCipher 256-bit AES Encryption: All your notes, research papers, Kanban tasks, and job dossiers are stored strictly on your local device storage inside a hardware-accelerated SQLCipher database.
  • Hardware KeyStore (TEE): Database passphrases and encryption master keys are derived and protected inside your Android device's hardware Trusted Execution Environment.
  • Zero-Knowledge Architecture: The developer has zero access to your database file or passphrases.

3. BYOK (Bring-Your-Own-Key) AI Reasoning Privacy

Pariyojana allows optional integration with AI reasoning providers (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Groq, DeepSeek, Ollama, Perplexity, OpenRouter) via your personal API keys. Requests are sent directly and securely over HTTPS from your device to the official endpoint. Decrypted keys exist in RAM only for active operational requests and are overwritten with zeroes (0x00) upon completion.

4. Crash Reporting & Anonymous Performance Metrics

To ensure application stability, Pariyojana uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymized crash stack traces and basic OS health telemetry. No Personally Identifiable Information (PII), project titles, or database contents are ever collected.

5. Device Permissions Rationale

Permission Requirement Rationale
Biometrics USE_BIOMETRIC To locally unlock your encrypted vault using fingerprint / face sensor.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS To deliver local study timers, focus alarms, and app update alerts.
Storage / Media READ_MEDIA_* To attach research PDFs, dossiers, or reference photos to projects.
Internet INTERNET To query open research metadata (ArXiv, Hacker News) and BYOK AI models.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

Since all data lives on your device, you have complete sovereignty. You can delete all your data instantly at any time via "Factory Reset Vault" in Settings or Android App Info.

7. Open Source Verification & Contact

Pariyojana is 100% open source under the MIT License. You can inspect the entire codebase on GitHub.
