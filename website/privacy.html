Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Official Domain: http://pariyojana.gt.tc/

1. Our Core Privacy Philosophy (Sovereignty)

Pariyojana (परियोजना) is built on the principle of personal data sovereignty. Your ideas, task lists, research notes, and personal projects belong exclusively to you. We collect ZERO analytics, ZERO telemetry, and ZERO personal identity data.

2. Local Database & Encryption

All user data is stored locally on your Android device inside an encrypted SQLCipher 256-bit AES database. Your API keys (OpenRouter, Gemini, Claude, GPT-4o, GitHub PAT) are hardware-encrypted in the Android KeyStore TEE (Trusted Execution Environment). Not even the app developer can access your keys or data.

3. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) & AI Processing

When you perform AI tasks (e.g., Idea Refinement, Resume Tailoring), Pariyojana connects directly from your device to the provider API using your provided API key. No intermediate telemetry proxy servers are used.

4. Optional Google Cloud Sync

If you enable Google Cloud Sync, database backups are uploaded exclusively to your personal Google Drive AppData hidden vault folder. Pariyojana servers never touch or store your backup files.

5. Open Source Transparency

Pariyojana is open source. You can audit our full codebase on GitHub anytime:
