Why Is It Named Pariyojana (परियोजना)?

Derived from ancient Sanskrit, Pariyojana(परि + योजना) means "A Plan of Noble Action & Structured Project".

परियोजना

The Meaning of Pariyojana

In Sanskrit, Pariyojana represents the transformation of a vision into reality through systematic planning. We chose this name because every great achievement starts as an unorganized idea that needs structure and dedicated effort.

विचार रक्षा

Cherishing & Nurturing Ideas

Your creative thoughts are sacred seeds. Instead of letting them disappear in random notepad drafts, Pariyojana provides a dedicated encrypted vault where ideas are cherished, organized, and promoted into active projects with 1 tap.

योगः कर्मसु

Sanskrit Wisdom & Deep Focus

Incorporates ancient Indian focus philosophy ("Yoga is excellence in action" — BG 2.50). The integrated Pomodoro timer displays 15 curated Sanskrit shlokas to calm your mind and help you maintain deep focus during work sprints.
